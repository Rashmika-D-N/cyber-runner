CYBER RUNNER
Created by Rashmika Nishshanka

WINDOWS 10/11, 64-BIT

Portable download: extract the ENTIRE ZIP into a folder, then open Cyber Runner.exe.
Keep the other files and folders alongside the EXE. Do not run it inside the ZIP.
Installer download: open the Setup EXE, install, then use the desktop shortcut.
No Node.js, browser installation, account, internet or asset download is required.

PLAY
Choose Start round. A/D or Left/Right change lane; Space/Up jumps; S/Down ducks.
P pauses or resumes. Enter starts a round. F11 toggles full screen; Escape exits it.

CAMERA
Enable the camera to use Body movement. Set the tracking area to exclude spectators.
Align the lane guides with three marked floor lanes. Stand in the middle until
Player locked appears, then hold a hand beside your face for one second to start.
Lower your hand before another hold. Feet do not need to remain visible.
Start manually also works with no player visible and with the camera off.
Windows Settings > Privacy & security > Camera must allow desktop apps to use it.
Camera frames stay on your device. The app blocks external network requests.

EXHIBITION / TWO SCREENS
Connect the second screen and choose Extend (Win+P).
Select Separate screens, or press Ctrl+Shift+E.
With two monitors, the game moves to the external display and controls to the
primary display. Move them manually if Windows has a different primary display.
Keep both windows visible. Use Full screen on the game window.
Closing controls pauses play. Closing the main game closes the application.
If gameplay is slow, choose Performance graphics in the controls.
